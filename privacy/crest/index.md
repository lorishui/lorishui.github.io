Crest helps you understand your daily energy and sleep rhythm using Apple Health data that stays on your device.

- **Effective Date:** May 21, 2026
- **Last Updated:** May 21, 2026
- **App:** Crest for iOS

> **Crest does not sell your data, does not use your health data for advertising or tracking, and does not upload your Apple Health data to developer-controlled servers.**
>
> The app reads selected Apple Health data only after you grant permission. The data is used to calculate daily energy, show sleep insights, and calibrate your own sleep rhythm.

## Information Crest Reads

With your permission, Crest may read the following data from Apple Health:

- **Sleep Analysis**, including sleep duration and available sleep stages.
- **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)**, when available.
- **Resting Heart Rate**, when available.
- **Step Count**, used only as supporting context.

Crest may also store your in-app subjective check-ins, such as how you felt after waking up.

## How Crest Uses This Information

- To calculate a daily energy score.
- To show sleep duration, sleep stage, HRV, and step context inside the app.
- To help you compare sleep patterns with how you feel after waking up.
- To improve local rhythm insights based on your own historical records.

Crest is for personal wellness and self-reflection. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

## Data Storage and Sharing

Crest stores app records locally on your device using Apple's local persistence technologies. Apple Health data remains protected by Apple Health permissions.

**Crest does not operate a backend server for collecting your health data.** Crest does not sell, rent, or share your health data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party analytics services.

If a future version adds optional account, backup, cloud sync, or third-party services, this policy will be updated before those features are used.

## Apple Health Permissions

You control Apple Health access. You can change or revoke Crest's Health permissions at any time:

- Open the **Health** app on your iPhone.
- Go to **Sharing** or **Data Access & Devices**.
- Select **Crest**.
- Turn off any data type you no longer want Crest to access.

After access is revoked, Crest will no longer be able to read those Apple Health data types.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local Crest records remain on your device unless you delete them in the app, clear local data through available app controls, or uninstall the app. Apple Health data is managed by Apple Health and your iOS settings.

## Children's Privacy

Crest is intended for general wellness use and is not directed to children. Crest does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-controlled servers.

## International Use

Crest may be used by people in different countries or regions. The app is designed so health data processing happens on the user's device unless a future version clearly introduces optional cloud features.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Crest changes how it handles data. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

For privacy questions about Crest, please contact the developer at [crest@imggenhub.org](mailto:crest@imggenhub.org).

## Crest 隐私政策（中文说明）

Crest 使用 Apple Health 数据帮助你理解每日能量和睡眠节奏。健康数据在你的设备上处理。

### 概要

**Crest 不会出售你的数据，不会把健康数据用于广告或追踪，也不会把 Apple Health 数据上传到开发者控制的服务器。**

### Crest 读取哪些信息

在你授权后，Crest 可能从 Apple Health 读取以下数据：

- **睡眠分析**：包括睡眠时长和可用的睡眠分期。
- **HRV 心率变异性**：如 Apple Health 中有可用数据。
- **静息心率**：如 Apple Health 中有可用数据。
- **步数**：仅作为状态解释的辅助参考。

Crest 也会在本机保存你在 App 内记录的主观状态，例如醒来后的感觉。

### 这些信息如何使用

- 计算每日能量分。
- 在 App 内展示睡眠时长、睡眠分期、HRV 和步数参考。
- 帮助你比较睡眠模式和醒来后的主观状态。
- 基于你自己的历史记录生成本地节奏洞察。

Crest 仅用于个人健康管理和自我观察，不是医疗设备，也不提供诊断、治疗或专业医疗建议。

### 数据存储与共享

Crest 使用 Apple 的本地持久化技术在你的设备上保存 App 记录。Apple Health 数据继续受 Apple Health 权限保护。

**Crest 当前不运行用于收集健康数据的开发者后台服务器。** Crest 不会向广告商、数据经纪商或第三方分析服务出售、出租或共享你的健康数据。

如果未来版本增加账号、备份、云同步或第三方服务，本隐私政策会在相关功能启用前更新。

### Apple Health 授权管理

你可以随时修改或撤销 Crest 的健康数据权限：

- 打开 iPhone 上的 **健康** App。
- 进入 **共享** 或 **数据访问与设备**。
- 选择 **Crest**。
- 关闭你不希望 Crest 继续访问的数据类型。

### 数据保留与删除

Crest 的本地记录会保留在你的设备上，直到你在 App 内删除、清空本地数据，或卸载 App。Apple Health 数据由 Apple Health 和 iOS 设置管理。

### 儿童隐私

Crest 面向一般健康管理场景，不专门面向儿童。Crest 不会通过开发者控制的服务器有意收集儿童个人信息。

### 国际用户

Crest 可供不同国家或地区的用户使用。除非未来版本明确加入可选云功能，健康数据处理会在用户设备上完成。

### 政策更新

当 Crest 的数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。最新版本会发布在本页面，并更新日期。

### 联系我们

如有 Crest 隐私相关问题，请通过 [crest@imggenhub.org](mailto:crest@imggenhub.org) 联系开发者。
