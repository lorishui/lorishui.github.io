Exif Privacy helps you inspect hidden photo metadata, remove private fields, and verify a clean copy before sharing.

- **Effective Date:** June 2, 2026
- **Last Updated:** June 2, 2026
- **App:** Exif Privacy for iOS

> **Exif Privacy does not sell your data, does not use your photos or metadata for advertising or tracking, and does not upload your selected photos or photo metadata to developer-controlled servers.**
>
> The app processes photos you choose on your device. Your original photo is not changed; Exif Privacy creates a separate clean copy for sharing or saving.

## Information Exif Privacy Handles

When you choose a photo, Exif Privacy may read the image file and its embedded metadata, including:

- **Location metadata**, such as GPS coordinates when present in the selected photo.
- **Capture time and camera information**, such as date, device, lens, exposure, aperture, ISO, focal length, and related EXIF fields when present.
- **Software, maker notes, and additional image metadata**, when present in the selected photo.
- **Technical image information**, such as file type, pixel size, and metadata field count.

Exif Privacy may also store small local app settings, such as whether onboarding has been completed, your selected app language, and a local clean-export count used for product testing.

## How Exif Privacy Uses This Information

- To scan a selected photo and show a privacy risk report.
- To preview photo location risk when GPS metadata exists.
- To create a clean copy with private metadata removed.
- To scan the clean copy again so you can verify what changed.
- To let you share the clean copy through the iOS system share sheet.
- To let you save the clean copy to your photo library if you grant permission.

Exif Privacy is a photo privacy utility. It does not provide backup, cloud storage, identity verification, or account services.

## Data Storage and Sharing

Photo scanning, metadata removal, and verification are performed on your device using iOS system frameworks. Exif Privacy does not operate a backend server for collecting your photos or photo metadata.

Your selected original photo remains in your photo library and is not modified by Exif Privacy. Cleaned images are held in the app while you use the workflow and may be shared or saved only when you choose those actions.

Exif Privacy does not sell, rent, or share your photos, photo metadata, or local app settings with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party analytics services.

If a future version adds optional account, backup, cloud sync, StoreKit purchases, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party services, this policy will be updated before those features are used.

## Photo Library Permissions

Exif Privacy uses the iOS photo picker so you can choose which photo to import. The app only receives the photo you select through the system picker.

If you choose to save a clean copy to Photos, Exif Privacy requests add-only photo library permission. This permission is used only to write the clean copy. You can change photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings:

- Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
- Go to **Privacy & Security**.
- Select **Photos**.
- Select **Exif Privacy**.
- Change or revoke the permission.

After access is revoked, Exif Privacy will no longer be able to save clean copies to your photo library unless you grant permission again.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local app settings remain on your device unless you reset them through available app or system controls, or uninstall the app.

Selected photo data and cleaned copies used inside the app workflow remain local to your device. Clean copies that you save to Photos are managed by the Photos app and your iOS settings. Clean copies that you share are handled by the destination apps or services you choose.

## Children's Privacy

Exif Privacy is not directed to children. Exif Privacy does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-controlled servers.

## International Use

Exif Privacy may be used by people in different countries or regions. The app is designed so photo processing happens on the user's device unless a future version clearly introduces optional cloud features.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Exif Privacy changes how it handles data. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

For privacy questions about Exif Privacy, please contact the developer at [exifprivacy@imggenhub.org](mailto:exifprivacy@imggenhub.org).

## Exif Privacy 隐私政策（中文说明）

Exif Privacy 帮助你查看照片中隐藏的元数据，移除可能暴露隐私的字段，并在分享前验证清理副本。

### 概要

**Exif Privacy 不会出售你的数据，不会把你的照片或元数据用于广告或追踪，也不会把你选择的照片或照片元数据上传到开发者控制的服务器。**

App 会在你的设备本地处理你主动选择的照片。你的原始照片不会被修改；Exif Privacy 会创建单独的清理副本，供你分享或保存。

### Exif Privacy 处理哪些信息

当你选择照片后，Exif Privacy 可能读取图片文件和其中嵌入的元数据，包括：

- **位置元数据**：例如照片中存在的 GPS 坐标。
- **拍摄时间和相机信息**：例如日期、设备、镜头、曝光、光圈、ISO、焦距及相关 EXIF 字段。
- **软件信息、厂商备注和其他图片元数据**：如照片中存在这些字段。
- **技术性图片信息**：例如文件类型、像素尺寸和元数据字段数量。

Exif Privacy 也可能在本机保存少量 App 设置，例如是否已完成引导、你选择的 App 语言，以及用于产品测试的本地清理导出次数。

### 这些信息如何使用

- 扫描你选择的照片，并展示隐私风险报告。
- 当照片包含 GPS 元数据时，预览位置隐私风险。
- 创建移除隐私元数据后的清理副本。
- 再次扫描清理副本，帮助你确认变更结果。
- 通过 iOS 系统分享面板分享清理副本。
- 在你授权后，将清理副本保存到照片图库。

Exif Privacy 是照片隐私工具，不提供备份、云存储、身份验证或账号服务。

### 数据存储与共享

照片扫描、元数据移除和结果验证都通过 iOS 系统框架在你的设备上完成。Exif Privacy 当前不运行用于收集照片或照片元数据的开发者后台服务器。

你选择的原始照片仍保留在照片图库中，Exif Privacy 不会修改原图。清理后的图片会在你使用流程时保留在 App 内，并且只有在你主动选择分享或保存时才会导出。

Exif Privacy 不会向广告商、数据经纪商或第三方分析服务出售、出租或共享你的照片、照片元数据或本地 App 设置。

如果未来版本增加可选账号、备份、云同步、StoreKit 购买、分析、崩溃报告或第三方服务，本隐私政策会在相关功能启用前更新。

### 照片图库权限

Exif Privacy 使用 iOS 系统照片选择器，让你选择要导入的照片。App 只会收到你通过系统选择器选择的照片。

如果你选择将清理副本保存到照片，Exif Privacy 会请求仅添加照片图库权限。该权限仅用于写入清理副本。你可以随时在 iOS 设置中修改照片权限：

- 打开 iPhone 上的 **设置**。
- 进入 **隐私与安全性**。
- 选择 **照片**。
- 选择 **Exif Privacy**。
- 修改或撤销权限。

撤销权限后，除非你再次授权，否则 Exif Privacy 将无法继续向照片图库保存清理副本。

### 数据保留与删除

本地 App 设置会保留在你的设备上，直到你通过可用的 App 或系统控制重置，或卸载 App。

App 流程中使用的已选照片数据和清理副本会保留在你的设备本地。你保存到照片图库的清理副本由照片 App 和 iOS 设置管理。你分享出去的清理副本由你选择的目标 App 或服务处理。

### 儿童隐私

Exif Privacy 不专门面向儿童。Exif Privacy 不会通过开发者控制的服务器有意收集儿童个人信息。

### 国际用户

Exif Privacy 可供不同国家或地区的用户使用。除非未来版本明确加入可选云功能，照片处理会在用户设备上完成。

### 政策更新

当 Exif Privacy 的数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。最新版本会发布在本页面，并更新日期。

### 联系我们

如有 Exif Privacy 隐私相关问题，请通过 [exifprivacy@imggenhub.org](mailto:exifprivacy@imggenhub.org) 联系开发者。
