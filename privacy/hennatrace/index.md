HennaTrace helps you trace henna designs using a live camera preview, built-in templates, and designs you choose to import.

- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Last Updated:** August 14, 2026
- **App:** HennaTrace for iOS

> **HennaTrace does not sell your data, does not use your camera frames or photos for advertising or tracking, and does not upload your selected photos, imported designs, or camera preview frames to developer-controlled servers.**
>
> Camera preview, imported design processing, and tracing reference exports are handled on your device. HennaTrace uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure in-app usage, but the current version does not use Analytics for cross-app tracking, targeted advertising, or data-broker sharing.

## Information HennaTrace Handles

HennaTrace may handle the following information when you use related features:

- **Camera preview frames**, used to show a live tracing view and create a tracing reference screenshot when you choose to save one.
- **Selected photos or image files**, when you choose to import your own design through the iOS photo picker.
- **Imported design images**, stored locally by the app so they can appear in My Designs.
- **Local app settings**, such as whether onboarding has been completed.
- **StoreKit purchase information**, handled by Apple to load products, purchase a subscription, restore purchases, and check active entitlements.
- **Analytics information**, collected by Google Analytics for Firebase, such as app usage events, event parameters, app version, operating system, device model, session information, and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier. Analytics may also derive approximate regional information from a masked IP address.

## How HennaTrace Uses This Information

- To show a live camera preview for tracing on your hand or skin.
- To overlay built-in templates or your imported designs on the camera preview.
- To process selected images into usable line art for tracing.
- To save imported designs locally in the app.
- To create a tracing reference screenshot and save it to Photos if you grant permission.
- To unlock Pro features through Apple's StoreKit subscription system.
- To understand how users interact with the app and improve HennaTrace using Google Analytics for Firebase.

The current version may send the following business events to Firebase Analytics: design views and selections, camera opens, overlay lock actions, completed trace exports, import actions, paywall views, and verified purchases. Event parameters are limited to app-defined metadata such as design identifiers, design names, design category/source, paywall source, and StoreKit product identifiers. HennaTrace does not send camera frames, selected photos, imported design image data, screenshots, email addresses, or other directly identifying information to Analytics.

HennaTrace is a creative tracing utility. It does not provide cloud backup, account services, identity verification, or medical advice.

## Data Storage and Sharing

Camera preview, image import processing, and tracing reference generation are performed on your device using iOS system frameworks.

Imported designs are saved locally in the app's Application Support storage. HennaTrace does not operate a backend server for collecting your camera frames, photos, imported designs, or local app settings.

HennaTrace does not sell, rent, or share your camera frames, photos, imported designs, or local app settings with advertisers or data brokers. Analytics event data is processed by Google through Firebase/Google Analytics as a third-party service. Google may process this data according to its applicable terms and privacy policies. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Subscription purchases and restores are processed by Apple StoreKit. Apple may handle purchase-related information according to Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

HennaTrace does not currently provide user accounts, cloud backup, cloud sync, or developer-operated storage for camera or image data. If future versions change these practices or add additional third-party services, this policy will be updated before the changed features are used.

## Camera and Photo Permissions

HennaTrace requests camera access to show the live tracing preview. Camera frames are used for the preview and for saving a tracing reference screenshot when you choose that action.

HennaTrace uses the iOS photo picker so you can choose an image to import. The app only receives the image you select through the system picker.

If you choose to save a tracing reference screenshot to Photos, HennaTrace requests add-only photo library permission. This permission is used only to write the screenshot. You can change camera and photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings:

- Open **Settings** on your iPhone.
- Go to **Privacy & Security**.
- Select **Camera** or **Photos**.
- Select **HennaTrace**.
- Change or revoke the permission.

After access is revoked, HennaTrace will no longer be able to use the related camera or photo feature unless you grant permission again.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local app settings and imported designs remain on your device unless you delete them through available app or system controls, or uninstall the app.

Tracing reference screenshots that you save to Photos are managed by the Photos app and your iOS settings. Images or screenshots that you share outside the app are handled by the destination apps or services you choose.

Firebase Analytics data is retained according to the retention and account settings configured for the HennaTrace Firebase project. Aggregated reports may continue to be retained after individual event data is no longer available. You may contact us to request deletion of analytics data associated with your installation where it can be identified and technically deleted. Because HennaTrace does not require an account or collect your name or email through the app, we may not be able to associate a request with a specific analytics installation in every case.

## Children's Privacy

HennaTrace is not directed to children. HennaTrace does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-controlled servers.

## International Use

HennaTrace may be used by people in different countries or regions. The app is designed so camera and photo processing happens on the user's device unless a future version clearly introduces optional cloud features.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when HennaTrace changes how it handles data. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

For privacy questions about HennaTrace, please contact the developer at [hennatrace@imggenhub.org](mailto:hennatrace@imggenhub.org).

## HennaTrace 隐私政策（中文说明）

HennaTrace 使用实时相机预览、内置模板和你主动导入的设计，帮助你进行海娜图案描摹。

### 概要

**HennaTrace 不会出售你的数据，不会把相机画面或照片用于广告或追踪，也不会把你选择的照片、导入设计或相机预览画面上传到开发者控制的服务器。**当前版本使用 Google Analytics for Firebase 统计 App 内使用情况，但不用于跨 App 追踪、定向广告或向数据经纪商共享。

相机预览、导入设计处理和描摹参考图导出都在你的设备本地完成。

### HennaTrace 处理哪些信息

HennaTrace 可能在你使用相关功能时处理以下信息：

- **相机预览画面**：用于显示实时描摹视图，并在你主动保存时生成描摹参考截图。
- **你选择的照片或图片文件**：当你通过 iOS 照片选择器导入自己的设计时使用。
- **导入的设计图片**：本地保存在 App 中，用于在 My Designs 中展示。
- **本地 App 设置**：例如是否已完成引导。
- **StoreKit 购买信息**：由 Apple 用于加载商品、购买订阅、恢复购买和检查有效权益。
- **分析信息**：由 Google Analytics for Firebase 收集，例如 App 使用事件、事件参数、App 版本、操作系统、设备型号、会话信息和伪匿名的 App 实例标识。Analytics 也可能根据经过掩码处理的 IP 地址推断大致地区信息。

### 这些信息如何使用

- 显示用于描摹的实时相机预览。
- 在相机预览上叠加内置模板或你导入的设计。
- 将你选择的图片处理成适合描摹的线稿。
- 在 App 本地保存导入设计。
- 在你授权后，将描摹参考截图保存到照片图库。
- 通过 Apple StoreKit 订阅系统解锁 Pro 功能。
- 通过 Google Analytics for Firebase 了解用户如何使用 App，并改进 HennaTrace。

当前版本可能向 Firebase Analytics 发送以下业务事件：设计展示与选择、打开相机、锁定图案、完成描摹导出、导入操作、展示订阅页和已验证的购买。事件参数仅包含 App 定义的元数据，例如设计 ID、设计名称、设计分类/来源、订阅页来源和 StoreKit 商品 ID。HennaTrace 不会把相机画面、选择的照片、导入设计图片内容、截图、邮箱或其他可直接识别个人的信息发送给 Analytics。

HennaTrace 是创作描摹工具，不提供云备份、账号服务、身份验证或医疗建议。

### 数据存储与共享

相机预览、图片导入处理和描摹参考图生成都通过 iOS 系统框架在你的设备上完成。

导入设计会保存在 App 的 Application Support 本地存储中。HennaTrace 当前不运行用于收集相机画面、照片、导入设计或本地 App 设置的开发者后台服务器。

HennaTrace 不会向广告商或数据经纪商出售、出租或共享你的相机画面、照片、导入设计或本地 App 设置。Analytics 事件数据会由 Google 作为第三方服务通过 Firebase/Google Analytics 处理。Google 可能根据适用的条款和隐私政策处理这些数据。详情请参阅 [Google 隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。

订阅购买和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。Apple 可能会根据其条款和隐私政策处理购买相关信息。

HennaTrace 当前不提供用户账号、云备份、云同步，也不运行用于保存相机或图片数据的开发者后台。如果未来版本改变这些做法或增加其他第三方服务，本隐私政策会在相关功能启用前更新。

### 相机和照片权限

HennaTrace 请求相机权限，用于显示实时描摹预览。相机画面用于预览，并在你主动选择保存时用于生成描摹参考截图。

HennaTrace 使用 iOS 系统照片选择器，让你选择要导入的图片。App 只会收到你通过系统选择器选择的图片。

如果你选择将描摹参考截图保存到照片，HennaTrace 会请求仅添加照片图库权限。该权限仅用于写入截图。你可以随时在 iOS 设置中修改相机和照片权限：

- 打开 iPhone 上的 **设置**。
- 进入 **隐私与安全性**。
- 选择 **相机** 或 **照片**。
- 选择 **HennaTrace**。
- 修改或撤销权限。

撤销权限后，除非你再次授权，否则 HennaTrace 将无法继续使用对应的相机或照片功能。

### 数据保留与删除

本地 App 设置和导入设计会保留在你的设备上，直到你通过可用的 App 或系统控制删除，或卸载 App。

你保存到照片图库的描摹参考截图由照片 App 和 iOS 设置管理。你分享到 App 外部的图片或截图由你选择的目标 App 或服务处理。

Firebase Analytics 数据按照 HennaTrace Firebase 项目中配置的保留期限和账号设置保存。单条事件数据到期后，聚合报告可能仍会保留。你可以联系我们，请求删除与你的安装实例相关的 Analytics 数据；如果该数据可以被识别且技术上可以删除，我们会处理相关请求。由于 HennaTrace 不要求创建账号，也不会通过 App 收集你的姓名或邮箱，因此在某些情况下可能无法将请求与特定的 Analytics 安装实例对应起来。

### 儿童隐私

HennaTrace 不专门面向儿童。HennaTrace 不会通过开发者控制的服务器有意收集儿童个人信息。

### 国际用户

HennaTrace 可供不同国家或地区的用户使用。除非未来版本明确加入可选云功能，相机和照片处理会在用户设备上完成。

### 政策更新

当 HennaTrace 的数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。最新版本会发布在本页面，并更新日期。

### 联系我们

如有 HennaTrace 隐私相关问题，请通过 [hennatrace@imggenhub.org](mailto:hennatrace@imggenhub.org) 联系开发者。
